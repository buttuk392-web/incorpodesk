Refund policy

Last updated: September 2026

Before work begins

If you cancel before IncorpoDesk has started work or incurred third-party costs, we will assess the request fairly and explain any available refund.

After work begins

Service fees may be partly non-refundable once work, document review, filing preparation or coordination has commenced. Government fees and third-party charges are generally non-refundable once incurred.

Failed or rejected filings

Government approval is not guaranteed. Where a filing is rejected for a correctable reason, we will discuss reasonable next steps; additional fees may apply depending on the cause.

Requesting a refund

Contact hello@incorpodesk.com with your order details and reason for the request. We aim to respond promptly.
